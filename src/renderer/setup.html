Connect Spotify

Play your playlists, liked songs, albums and podcasts on your iPod.

✓

Connected

Your Spotify account is linked.

  1. Create a Spotify app

    Spotify asks every app to have its own key. It takes a minute and is free.

    Sign in, click Create app, and give it any name (for example “My iPod”).

  2. Add this Redirect URI

    http://127.0.0.1:43827/callback

    Under “Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?” tick Web API and Web Playback SDK, then save.

  3. Paste your Client ID

    Open your app’s Settings and copy the Client ID. No secret needed.

Spotify Premium is required to control playback. Music plays through the Spotify app on this PC (or any Spotify Connect speaker) and your iPod becomes the remote — or straight from the iPod when your build supports it. If sign-in is refused, add your Spotify email under User Management in the dashboard.
