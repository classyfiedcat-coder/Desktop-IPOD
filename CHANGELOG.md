# Changelog

## 4.0.0

Five iPods, each built to real measurements.

- **Models** (Settings › Appearance › Model): original (2001), mini (2004), 5th generation (2005), classic (2007) and nano 3G (2007), in their real colours. Measured from Apple's specs and product images and iFixit's photos.
- **Bodies**: plastic under clear acrylic, anodised aluminium on steel, or the mini's aluminium tube. Thickness follows capacity (160GB classic: 13.5mm).
- **Controls and ports**: the original's wheel turns inside a ring of buttons, with FireWire and a white hold slider on top. The nano's ports are all on the bottom.
- **Screens**: monochrome Chicago LCDs on the original and mini; the 2007 look with panning album art on the classic and nano.
- **Click sounds** (Settings › Click Sound) and **album-coloured Now Playing** (Settings › Appearance › Now Playing).
- **5th generation corrected**: screen and wheel higher, smaller centre button, tighter corners, grey wheel with white labels on the white model.

## 3.1.0

- Closer 5th generation: real screen size, clear acrylic edge, satin wheel, 14mm-thick 60/80GB models, real back layout.
- The screen stays sharp when tilted or floating.
- Software Update explains failures in one line.

## 3.0.1

- A song picked from Spotify search keeps playing into a mix instead of stopping.

## 3.0.0

- **Mac app** (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 13+) with a menu bar, Dock and ⌃⌥⌘ shortcuts.
- **iTunes / Music library**: playlists, ratings, play counts and dates, read-only.
- **Spotify**: a song from search plays on its own, then your queue; results show art, artist, album, year and the explicit mark.
- **Updates install themselves** at a quiet moment; Settings › Software Update.
- Sharper screen at an angle.

## 2.0.0

- **3D body** (WebGL): turns toward the pointer, lifts, sways, floats and flips; reflective glass, plastic and steel; wear; Light detail fallback.
- **Music**: gapless and crossfade, 10-band EQ, Sound Check, speeds, bookmarks, Up Next, Cover Flow, your own playlists, song options, lyrics, artwork lookup, search.
- **Radio and podcasts**: radio-browser.info stations; podcast charts, search, OPML, downloads.
- **Spotify**: queue, playlists, device in Now Playing, search results.
- **Extras**: Solitaire, contacts, calendars, video categories, colours and engraving.
- **Desktop**: taskbar buttons, jump list, shortcuts, drag and drop, notifications, snapping, start at login, backups.
- Background library scanning, a safer network layer, lower CPU and GPU use, unit and end-to-end tests.

## 1.0.0

First release: a frameless 5th generation iPod with a click wheel, menus, Now Playing, local music, Spotify, games and extras.
